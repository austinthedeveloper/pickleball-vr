# Changelog

What's new in each release of Pickleball '94 VR, for players. The format
follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com); versions follow
[semver](https://semver.org) (design/RELEASING.md).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- COACH VIEW's panel on a computer has the phone's PRANKS tab: the sounds,
  the announcer's calls, the partner's antics, confetti, the silly ball,
  paddle and weather, and HOLD TO TALK (or hold T) through the computer's
  microphone, in the phone's four voices (ADR-029).

### Changed

- The headset's COACH row PRANKS FROM THE PHONE is now PRANKS FROM THE
  COACH: it covers a watching computer too.

### Fixed

- On a computer, a quick mouse click (or a trackpad tap) on a menu row now
  presses it; before, only a long press did.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-10-29

The coach-view update. Your coach can watch you play in 3D from anywhere,
run your drill from their own screen, and replay your last shot slowed
down from any angle.

### Added
- **WATCH A PLAYER:** a coach can watch you play from anywhere. They see
  your drill on the court in 3D: you, the partner or machine, and every
  shot, from behind you, the side, across the net or above. Turn on COACH
  FROM ANYWHERE; they pick WATCH A PLAYER on a Mac or a headset and enter
  your room. They coach from the panel on their screen: the drill and
  level, start and pause, cues, the target, the partner's next ball,
  the ball machine and the numbers. The phone page still works too.
- **Instant replay for the coach:** THE LAST SHOT again, from any camera,
  at full speed, half or a quarter, held on the contact, a frame at a
  time if they like. Any moment from the last minute.

### Changed
- **Your friend's body in PLAY A FRIEND** bends to a low ball, and holds
  their paddle by the grip as they really do.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-10-29

The play-a-friend update. Dink with a friend anywhere with the internet,
one on one, from your headset or a Mac.

### Added
- **PLAY A FRIEND:** dink with a friend anywhere with the internet, one
  on one. One of you hosts and gets a room (four letters); the other
  picks those letters and joins. FRIENDLY DINK has no serves to worry
  about and no score: drop the ball, dink, and the board counts the
  rally and keeps your best. The Mac version can play a headset too.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-29

The coach-from-anywhere update. Your coach can watch and talk to you from
anywhere with the internet, a more realistic practice partner, and a
better-looking court.

### Fixed
- The other players' paddle no longer floats away from their hand in some
  wind-ups.

### Added
- **COACH FROM ANYWHERE** on the COACH page: your coach can watch and
  coach from anywhere, not just your Wi-Fi. The headset shows a room
  (like ROOM BXKM); the coach opens the relay's page and types the room,
  with no code to enter. There, HOLD TO TALK uses the phone's microphone, iPhones
  included.
- **PARTNER LOOK** on PRACTICE PARTNER puts a more realistic person
  across the net, from Microsoft's free Rocketbox library:
  - NEW MAN, in a striped polo and cargo shorts;
  - NEW WOMAN, in a hoodie, shorts and sneakers;
  - CLASSIC, the partner you had.

### Changed
- **A real net:** square mesh you can see through, the white tape over
  the top, a centre strap, and metal posts.
- **The court's surroundings:** the court has a sand-grain finish, the
  grass has texture, the fence is chain link with dark windscreens behind
  the baselines, and the trees are fuller.
- **The crowd are people:** seated in their own shirts, with arms, legs
  and hair, and they throw their arms up for a great rally.
- **A paddle that looks like one:** a carbon face with a colour panel and
  a "94", a proper edge and a wound grip.

## [0.4.1] - 2026-10-28

A fix for the pranks: voice messages from an iPhone get through.

### Fixed
- **PRANKS on an iPhone:** a voice message recorded as a video now goes

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-28

The pranks update. Hand the coach's phone to a kid and let them loose:
sounds, silly balls and paddles, weather, a dancing partner, their voice
in your ears and their face on the partner.

### Added
- **PRANKS:** hand the coach's phone to a kid. A soundboard (airhorn,
  boo, sad trombone, whoopee...), the announcer calling OUT! on a good
  ball, silly balls (a rubber duck that squeaks, a watermelon that
  splats, their face) and paddles (a frying pan that clangs, a baguette,
  a fly swatter, a foam #1 hand), snow, bubbles and confetti, a partner
  who dances and flexes, their voice in your headset (robot, chipmunk or
  monster), and their selfie on the partner's face. Pause and pick
  MERCY for a minute's peace; COACH > PRANKS FROM THE PHONE turns it off.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-28

The coaching update. A coach follows along on a phone, places targets
and feeds, runs the ball machine and saves every session; pause a drill
and the same panel stands beside you in the headset. The partner has a
real body, targets are dartboards, passthrough shows your room, and
timed runs keep records.

### Added
- **The coach tablet in the headset:** pause a practice drill and the
  coaching panel stands beside you. It shows your run, where your shots
  landed and what the numbers say. Point at it to place the target, the
  next feed, the machine and its bounce, or to set and start the
  machine.
- **The coach's ball machine:** from the phone, start the machine, place
  it anywhere on their side, tap where its balls should bounce, and set
  their speed, spin, height over the net and how often they come.
- **Coaching, much more** (design/COACHING.md):
  - **Placement:** the coach taps to place the target or choose where the
    partner's next ball goes, and draws zones and lines that glow on
    your court.
  - **Messages:** cues ("PADDLE UP") and challenges ("8 in a row on the
    board") appear over the net.
  - **During the drill:** live pace and target-size sliders, and
    workouts that run several drills back to back.
  - **Per shot:** net clearance, forehand/backhand, contact height,
    ready position, footwork and a sweet-spot map.
  - **Analysis:** plain-English insights, rally replays from above and
    the side, and a printable report.
  - **History:** every session is saved on the headset with history and
    trends, and each player has their own profile and records.
- **Coach companion:** turn on MAIN > COACH, and a coach opens the address
  it shows on a phone on the same Wi-Fi, then enters the code. They see
  your score, clock and streak live, where every shot landed, a session
  summary by drill and your records. They can set up the drill and
  start, pause, restart or end it.
- **Timed runs:** a MODE row on PRACTICE PARTNER and BALL MACHINE. FREE
  PLAY is as before; 1, 2 or 3 MIN give you a clock and a score (good
  balls plus dartboard points). RECORDS shows your best and latest runs
  for each drill, so you can see yourself improve.
- **Passthrough:** PASSTHROUGH on MAIN shows your room around the court.
  Only the court, the net, the players and the scoreboard are drawn.
- **The partner and the CPU players have real bodies:** a full 3D person
  instead of shapes, in a real T-shirt and shorts, with shoes and hair,
  moving just as before. In doubles, the ladder's players are men and
  women.
- **Targets are dartboards:** bigger, with an outer ring (1 point), an
  inner ring (2) and a bullseye (3). Anywhere on the board counts, and
  the scoreboard keeps your POINTS.
- **Four new kitchen drills** on PRACTICE PARTNER:
  - **CALL THE SPOT:** a green spot lights on their side for each ball;
    dink onto it. It lights later and gets smaller the harder you set it.
  - **LINE AND CROSS:** only one half of their kitchen counts, straight
    ahead, cross court, or alternating.
  - **VOLLEY DINKS:** soft floaters at your kitchen line; volley them
    onto the spot without stepping in.
  - **ATTACK OR DINK:** speed up the dinks that sit up, dink the rest.
- **RESETS:** a new PRACTICE PARTNER drill. You stand mid-court and the
  partner drives at you, hard and low. Block it soft into their kitchen,
  under the red pop-up line over the net. DIFFICULTY sets the pace and
  how low the line is.
- **PADDLE** (on MAIN), with profiles for paddle accessories. DEFAULT is
  the paddle you had. PROFILE 1-3 set the ANGLE (-30 to 90), TWIST and
  REACH (how far out the face sits) to fit an accessory, and the paddle
  in your hand moves as you change them.
- **HANDS:** a new PRACTICE PARTNER drill (DRILL: HANDS). You and the
  partner stand at your kitchen lines and volley hard at each other; it
  takes every ball out of the air and sends it back at you. DIFFICULTY
  is the pace.

### Changed
- **Full swings hit full:** a big drive from the baseline now carries
  over the net. Touch and dinks are unchanged.
- **The coach tablet shows the drill's own controls:** pace and size in
  a partner drill, the machine's in the ball machine. Each new drill
  starts with a clean court, and a tap shows at once and says what it
  did.
- **Checking for paddle drift:** PADDLE > CHECK reads the paddle laid
  flat on the floor, live. The swing log records tracking quality for
  every hit.
- **Coach: START NEW** starts the drill the coach just set, mid-run.
- **Stronger hit vibration** (raised twice), and a light **ding** when a
  ball lands on a target, higher toward the bullseye.
- **Paddle profiles turn any way:** TWIST goes a full turn, ANGLE runs
  from -90 to 90, and a new TURN swings the face left or right. That
  covers paddle accessories that only hold the controller one way.
  REACH goes negative (down to -30 cm, in 1 cm steps) for accessories
  that hold the controller up the handle.
- **The DINKS target follows you:** it sits in front of the partner,
  moves to the other half when you switch sides, and moves after a miss
  as well as a hit.
- **Leaning over the kitchen line isn't a fault in practice:** you're
  only in when your head is 45 cm past the line.
- DINK PARTNER is now PRACTICE PARTNER, with DINKS and HANDS drills.
- PADDLE ANGLE moved from MATCH SETUP to the PADDLE page, so it's
  reachable from practice too.
- **HANDS starts each rally with a soft feed** straight at you, then
  speeds up quickly to the DIFFICULTY's pace, which is a little harder
  than before.
- **A bigger, unhurried swing** no longer sends a dink long: the middle
  of the swing range is a little softer. Gentle touch and full drives
  are unchanged.
- **The dink zone** now runs 2.5 ft past the kitchen line.
- **FOCUS is much stronger:** it starts at 5 in a row and the world is
  nearly black around the court by 13.
- **Blocks keep more pace:** stick the paddle out at a hard-hit ball and
  it goes back with up to 72% of its speed.
- **Every partner feed has a lead-in:** the partner holds the next ball
  up, drops it and hits it as it falls, so you see where it's coming
  from and when.

### Fixed
- **Menu arrows both work:** clicking the < on a setting steps it back,
  the > steps it on.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-27

The practice update: a dinking partner, a paddle tuned on real strokes,
and a world that falls away in a long rally.

### Added
- **DINK PARTNER:** a CPU at their kitchen line who only wants the rally
  to go on. Every ball comes back as a dink to your forehand or backhand.
  - **DIFFICULTY** sets how low it skims the net.
  - **PARTNER** puts it straight across or cross court.
  - **SPREAD** sets how far it makes you reach.
  - **Floor targets** to aim at.
  - The board counts dinks in a row. A dink landing up to a foot past
    their kitchen line counts.
- **FOCUS:** string together a long rally and everything outside the court
  darkens, step by step, a spotlight on the rally. In practice it begins
  at your 8th good shot in a row.
- **The ball machine:**
  - At your kitchen line, drives come at you through the air for reflex
    practice.
  - New **SPEED** (up to about 17 m/s), **MACHINE AT** (the baseline or
    their kitchen line) and **RAPID** rate settings.
  - Real dinks from their kitchen.
  - Floor targets.
- **A new look:** the partner and the CPU players are people now. They
  split-step, shuffle, watch the ball and swing in time with the hit. The
  ball machine has a hopper that empties, a head that aims along each
  feed and a countdown light. The paddle and the ball (with holes) are
  remade, and fast balls leave a short trail.

### Changed
- **The paddle feels like a paddle.** It was tuned on 160+ logged strokes
  from a real player:
  - lifting through the ball lifts it;
  - touch shots are livelier;
  - an average swing gives a firm, controlled ball instead of flying long;
  - the sweet spot is gentler.
- **SHOT ASSIST** helps with length more than aim, so you keep your
  direction.
- **The partner** reads your shot and moves before it bounces, and its
  footwork carries its body.

### Fixed
- Dinks from the machine were called OUT before your return came down.
- Drives at the kitchen line came as slow lobs.
- In the headset, the lobby's demo took your controllers, reset you to a
  corner each point and hid the menu.
- Changing an option no longer shifts your view. Holding the Meta button
  recenters you facing the court.
- The ball in your free hand sits in your fingers and leaves from there.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-27

The first release: first-person pickleball on the Meta Quest, with a
practice mode built for the kitchen line.

### Added
- **Singles and doubles** against the remaster's eight ladder teams, from
  HENDERSON to the CHAMPS, each with its own style. Rally scoring to 5
  (up to 15, win by 2 optional), the two-bounce rule, and no volleys from
  the kitchen.
- **Your paddle is the paddle:** swing it, angle it, brush the ball for
  topspin or slice. A shot assist (OFF, LOW, MID, FULL) helps with
  length; you keep the aim. PADDLE HAND and PADDLE ANGLE fit it to you.
- **DINK PARTNER:** a CPU at their kitchen line who only wants the rally
  to go on. Every ball comes back as a dink near you. EASY, MEDIUM or HARD
  (how low it skims the net), across or cross court, with floor targets
  to aim at. The board counts dinks in a row.
- **BALL MACHINE:** drives, dinks, lobs or a mix, at four speeds, from the
  baseline or their kitchen line. At your kitchen line, drives come at
  you through the air for reflex practice. There are return zones,
  targets and a best streak.
- **HOW TO PLAY:** seven short lessons, from your first hits to topspin.
- **Five venues:** PARK, LAKESIDE, SUNSET, GYM and SPACE, each with the
  remaster's court colours and sounds. HOME plays at your opponent's
  court.
- **The remaster's sounds:** shot-by-shot hits, the announcer calling the
  score and the faults, and a crowd that stands for long rallies.
- **Flat mode** for playing on a Mac or PC without a headset.

### Known issues
- Builds are signed with a development key: install by sideloading
  (developer mode).
- The art is placeholder: simple shapes, no textures yet.
