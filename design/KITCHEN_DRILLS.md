# Kitchen drills: proposals

The lead plays the other VR pickleball games: one has a single dinking
mode with targets that move each shot, the other nothing. Filling that
kitchen gap is why this app exists. These are partner drills where the
partner feeds and you have to put the ball somewhere. Each one reuses
what's built: the partner (ADR-010), HANDS (ADR-014), floor targets,
zones, the streak and FOCUS.

## 1. CALL THE SPOT (built: ADR-017)

The partner dinks to you. As each ball crosses the net, a spot on their
side lights up: their forehand or backhand corner of the kitchen, at
their feet, or down the middle. Put your dink there.
- Score: hits on the lit spot in a row. A dink in but off the spot keeps
  the rally going and resets the streak.
- Difficulty: how early the spot lights (as they hit, at the net, after
  the bounce) and how small it is.
- Why it's better than moving targets: you choose after you see the ball,
  like a real point.

## 2. LINE AND CROSS (built: ADR-017)

Skinny dinking. The partner stands across or cross court; only half
their kitchen counts, as in skinny singles. A setting picks the lane:
LINE, CROSS, or ALTERNATE (line, cross, line...).
- Score: dinks in the lane in a row.
- Teaches: changing direction, and the cross-court dink's longer, safer
  path.

## 3. THIRD SHOT DROPS

You start at your baseline. The partner, at their kitchen line, feeds a
deep return. Drop it into their kitchen, then follow it in. They dink
back to you wherever you've reached, and you finish at the kitchen line.
- Score: drops that land in the kitchen (or the dink zone) in a row,
  with a bonus if you reach your line by their next ball.
- Difficulty: the return's depth and pace.

## 4. RESETS (built: ADR-016)

You stand in the transition zone, mid-court. The partner drives at you,
hard and low, like a speed-up. Block it soft into their kitchen.
- Score: resets that land in the dink zone in a row. A pop-up the
  partner can attack (above a line at the net) doesn't count, and HARD
  sees them put it away.
- It builds on the block physics just added.

## 5. ATTACK OR DINK (built: ADR-017)

The partner dinks, but some come up high. Speed up the high ones (hard
at their body) and dink the low ones.
- Score: right decisions in a row. On EASY, a ball high enough to attack
  glows for a moment, so you learn what one looks like.
- Teaches: patience, the hardest kitchen skill.

## 6. VOLLEY DINKS (built: ADR-017)

The partner dinks a little higher, landing near your kitchen line. Take
each one out of the air (no bounce, and not from inside the kitchen)
and dink it into a lit spot.
- Score: volley dinks on the spot in a row.

## Suggested order

CALL THE SPOT first: it's the most direct answer to the other game's
mode, and the rest can reuse its spots. Then RESETS and THIRD SHOT DROPS
(the transition game), then ATTACK OR DINK.
